Significant Figures Problem Set (10 Questions)

Part A – Identifying Significant Figures (Questions 1–4)

Determine the number of significant figures in each measurement.

  1. 0.00456 m
  2. 7000 g
  3. 3.040 × 10³ L
  4. 0.02010 kg

Part B – Rounding to Correct Significant Figures (Questions 5–7)

Round the following numbers to the specified number of significant figures.

  1. 0.006742 to 3 significant figures
  2. 14567 to 2 significant figures
  3. 9.8765 × 10⁻³ to 2 significant figures

Part C – Calculations with Significant Figures (Questions 8–10)

Perform the calculation and give your answer with the correct number of significant figures.

  1. 12.11 + 0.023 + 3.1 = ?
  2. 4.56 × 1.4 = ?
  3. (0.00452 × 3.2) ÷ 0.02 = ?

Answer Key

  1. 3

  3. 4
  4. 4
