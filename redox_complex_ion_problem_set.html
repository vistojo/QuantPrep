Redox & Complex Ion Equilibrium Problem Set (4 Questions)

Instructions:

Solve the following redox and complex ion equilibrium problems. Show all calculations, including determining the relevant equilibrium constants or cell potentials.

Problems:

  1. Calculate the standard cell potential (E°cell) for the reaction: Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s). Given E°(Cu²⁺/Cu) = +0.34 V and E°(Zn²⁺/Zn) = -0.76 V.
  2. Determine the equilibrium constant (K) for the reaction: Fe²⁺ + Ce⁴⁺ → Fe³⁺ + Ce³⁺ at 25°C using the standard potentials E°(Ce⁴⁺/Ce³⁺) = +1.61 V and E°(Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺) = +0.77 V.
  3. Calculate the concentration of Cu²⁺ in a solution at equilibrium if 0.10 M NH₃ is added to form the complex [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺, given Kf = 1.0 × 10¹³.
  4. For the reaction Ag⁺ + 2 NH₃ ⇌ [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺, calculate the concentration of free Ag⁺ in a solution where [NH₃] = 0.05 M and Kf = 1.6 × 10⁷.

Answer Key

  1. E°cell = 1.10 V
  2. K ≈ 1.4 × 10¹⁴
  3. [Cu²⁺] ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ M
  4. [Ag⁺] ≈ 6.25 × 10⁻⁸ M
